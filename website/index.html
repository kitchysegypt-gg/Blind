Marketing & Sales Strategy

  1. Free to start, paid from 250 EGPFree with family helpers; paid plans add our helpers and professionals.

  4. Pharmacies sign up their eldersThey set up the app and become the first helpers.
  5. NGOs and clinicsGroups and clinics for older people.
