Marketing & Sales Strategy

  1. Free, Plus and PremiumFree with family helpers; Plus from 250 EGP adds Talabat and pharmacy delivery; Premium from 450 EGP adds Alfa Lab home tests and home help.
  2. Partners pay per orderPharmacies and shops pay a small fee per order.
  3. Ads to Egyptians abroadFacebook, Instagram and TikTok.
  4. Pharmacies sign up their eldersThey set up the app and become the first helpers.
  5. NGOs and clinicsGroups and clinics for older people.
  6. Every helper link is a free adEach reply link and QR code shows the app to someone new.
