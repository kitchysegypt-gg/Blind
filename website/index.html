Helping Hand Business Plan

Market Opportunity

01

Egypt first: about 9 million people aged 60+, plus several hundred thousand blind and low-vision adults. Almost all can be reached by smartphone and WhatsApp.

02

Then the region and the world: about 30 million people aged 60+ in the Arab world, and 2.2 billion people with a vision impairment.

Why now: most older Egyptians already own a smartphone and use WhatsApp daily; phones have good built-in speech and cameras; more children live in another city or abroad; and delivery, pharmacy and ride services keep growing but aren't built for people who can't see the screen.

Problem Statement

Blind and elderly people depend on others for everyday tasks, but today's apps were built for young people who can see a screen. Delivery, pharmacy and ride apps rely on small buttons, maps and long menus, and most work in English first.

The result: lost independence, loneliness, and a heavy, disorganised load on families.

01

Groceries, medicine, rides and reading letters all need sight or mobility. Few older people can use a screen reader.

02

Requests for help go through scattered calls and voice notes. Nobody knows if help is coming.

03

A missed dose or getting lost can become an emergency, and families far away can't tell.

Our Solution

Helping Hand is a voice-first phone app in Arabic and English with a few large buttons. Say what you need and it opens the right screen.

It works with VoiceOver and TalkBack, and opens straight from the web, with no app store needed.

01

Ask for help: groceries, medicine, a ride or a helper. The helper replies with one tap ("I'm on my way"), and the app reads the answer aloud.

02

Read text: photograph a letter or medicine box and hear it. Medicine reminders speak at the right time.

03

Where am I? speaks your street address. One-tap family calls and an emergency button that shares your location.

Value Proposition

01.

For users: ask for anything by voice, in your own language, and know help is coming.

02.

For families: know your parent is safe, took their medicine and has help, even from abroad.

03.

For partners: reach a large, underserved group of customers through an app they can actually use.

04.

Our edge: Arabic voice first, big simple buttons, help from people you trust, no install for helpers.

Target Audience

Our users are older people living alone and blind or low-vision adults. Their families usually set the app up and pay for it.
